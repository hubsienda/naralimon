There is an extremely effective way to make someone want to do something:

tell them not to do it.

A normal button is just a button.

A button labelled **DO NOT PRESS** is now a personal challenge.

There is even a name for part of what is happening: psychological reactance. When people feel their freedom to choose is being restricted, a tiny rebellion can appear.

Which may explain why nobody particularly cares about a lift button until somebody puts a sign over it saying:

**OUT OF ORDER. DO NOT PRESS.**

At which point pressing it becomes essential.
