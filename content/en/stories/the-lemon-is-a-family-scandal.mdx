You look at a lemon and think:

lemon.

Simple.

Not remotely.

The familiar lemon has a complicated hybrid ancestry involving citron and sour orange. Sour orange itself carries ancestry from mandarin and pummelo.

So the lemon family tree looks slightly less like a tree and rather more like several people saying:

“This is getting complicated.”

Oranges, mandarins, lemons, pummelos and their relatives have been mixing genetic histories for a very long time.

So the next time somebody refers to something as “just a lemon”, you may correct them.

It isn't simple.

**It has a past.**
