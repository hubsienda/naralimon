One slice remains.

Four people have noticed it.

Nobody moves.

Diplomatic negotiations begin.

“Does anybody want the last piece?”

“No, no. You have it.”

“I’m full.”

A lie.

Everyone wants the pizza.

But everyone also wants to appear generous, civilised and morally superior to the other people who clearly want the pizza.

So a small strategic game begins: acquire the slice without looking like the person who desperately wanted the slice.

Eventually someone says:

“Well, if nobody wants it…”

Gone.

Humanity has invented laws, parliaments and international treaties.

We have still not solved **the last slice of pizza**.
